Aurelia:
space to slow down.

A fictional luxury short-stay concept designed to present a curated collection of homes with a calm, editorial feel and a straightforward path to explore and enquire.

HTMLCSSJavaScript

Aurelia is a self-initiated portfolio project. I designed and built a sample hospitality experience that brings together property discovery, destination and date inputs, editorial brand storytelling, and an enquiry form.

Goal

Develop a polished concept website that helps visitors browse a small collection of illustrative stays and understand the attentive, considered experience behind the brand.

My role

I developed the fictional brand direction and built the front end, including page structure, visual styling, responsive behavior, collection filters, search interactions, and the enquiry experience.

Design & build decisions

A restrained green and warm neutral palette, refined serif display type, and large property imagery create an editorial mood. The page moves from the collection promise to searchable stays, property cards, brand story, and an enquiry form.

Challenges & focus

The key design focus was making discovery controls feel simple without letting them compete with the imagery. Clear labels, grouped property cards, and a distinct enquiry section help make the browsing path easier to follow.

What I learned

This project gave me practice organizing a multi-part hospitality page and connecting filtering and search-style interactions to a consistent visual language. It also reinforced the importance of clearly identifying illustrative content.

Project notes

All properties, locations, prices, and availability are fictional. The enquiry form is a demonstration and does not make a real booking.

Transparency: Aurelia is a fictional portfolio concept; its property details and booking experience are illustrative only.
