Field & Fire:
a neighborhood table.

A sample restaurant website concept focused on seasonal food, warm hospitality, and making the essentials—story, menu, visit information, and a reservation request—easy to explore.

HTMLCSSJavaScript

This is a fictional portfolio project, not a commissioned restaurant website. I designed and built it as a way to practice presenting a hospitality brand through a complete, responsive web experience.

Goal

Create a welcoming restaurant concept site that gives visitors a quick sense of the atmosphere and a clear path to explore the sample menu or make a reservation request.

My role

I created the concept and built the front-end experience, including the page structure, visual styling, responsive layout, and interactive menu and form behaviors.

Design & build decisions

A deep green and warm neutral palette, editorial serif headings, food photography, and generous spacing support the intended intimate dining mood. The page groups the story, menu, visit details, and reservation form into a clear flow. Menu categories use interactive tabs.

Challenges & focus

The main design challenge was balancing an atmospheric, image-led presentation with practical information. I kept key actions visible and used clear labels and page sections so the experience remains understandable on smaller screens.

What I learned

This project helped me practice building a consistent visual system across a long landing page and thinking through how interactive details—such as menu tabs and form feedback—fit into the overall user journey.

Project notes

The restaurant, menu, prices, and reservation experience are illustrative. The demo form does not create a real booking.
